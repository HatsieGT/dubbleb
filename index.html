Our first game

      
BL0B

      
A 20-player PC game about a city under attack from within. Sane soldiers defend it. Corrupt soldiers, former Sane, want to turn everyone else. When the 15-minute timer ends, the team with the most players wins.

      

        

          
Sane

          
Normal soldiers defending their city.

          
    
            
  • UC Rays from your gun cure Corrupt players
    • 
            
  • Earn Cores to buy personal upgrades
    •

    • 
            
  • Led from HQ by King Sanitation

The rules

      
How it plays

      

        
One quest per team
Corrupt nodes spawn more Corrupt into the city. Sane players disable a node by entering a random code from 1 to 20.

        
Upgrades
Sane unlock better guns. Corrupt unlock laser eyes (or something similar) that hit watches from range. Team upgrades are pooled, and player upgrades come from your own balance.

        
Fair income
Income scales with team size. We're tuning it so the smaller team never gets starved.

        
Instant respawn
You respawn right away, so there is no kill-cam and no spectator mode. You stay in the fight.
